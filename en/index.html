Digital solutions that truly fit.

Digitalization that takes work off your hands.

Websites, online shops and custom tools for freelancers, small businesses and local trades. I start by looking at where things get stuck for you – then build what fits. From Schwarzenbek near Hamburg, across Germany.

Hi, I'm Julian.

I'm a freelance developer based in Schwarzenbek near Hamburg, working with freelancers, small businesses and local trades – people with no IT department of their own who still need something that fits.

Whether it's a website, an online shop, a small program or a workflow that should get simpler: I listen first, sort the plan out with you, then build it. You deal with one person throughout – no hotline, no ticket number, no changing contacts.

Off-the-shelf software makes you adapt to it. A good tool works the other way round. So I start by asking where your day actually gets stuck, and only build afterwards. Sometimes the honest answer is that it isn't worth building at all.

How I work.

No rigid process. The weight shifts with the job – sometimes it starts with a sketch, sometimes with a look at what's already there. The four steps below are the usual frame, not a corset.

  1. 01

    Listening

    To begin with

    You tell me where things get stuck. I keep asking until I understand how you work – and say honestly whether building something is worth it.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Depends on scope

    What is genuinely needed, which route makes sense, what does it cost? You get something you can read and decide on, before any budget moves.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    As agreed

    I build it and show you where it stands along the way. You see early how it's shaping up – and can change course while that's still cheap.

  4. 04

    Support

    If you want it

    Handover, a walkthrough, and afterwards maintenance and changes if you'd like them. Either way I stay your point of contact.

Common questions.

What gets asked most often before anyone gets in touch — answered here.

I don't know exactly what I need yet. Can we still talk?

Yes, that's the normal case. It's enough if you can describe what gets in your way day to day — what comes of it we work out together.

What's the first step?

Tell me in two sentences where things are getting stuck. I usually reply within 24 hours and ask follow-up questions.

What does the price depend on?

On scope, and on how clearly the job is defined. Clearly scoped work at a fixed price, otherwise a budget with a ceiling.

How long does something like this take?

A manageable website usually two to four weeks, a small tool often a few days, an online shop four to ten weeks.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A spreadsheet template, a report or a few changes are all fine — not every piece of work has to be a project.

Can you take over an existing website?

Often, yes. I look at what it's built on, then tell you honestly whether maintaining, reworking or rebuilding is cheaper.

Do you keep looking after it afterwards?

If you want — updates, small changes, new content. Either as needed or as a monthly allowance; it isn't compulsory.

Do I need a website or an online shop?

That depends on your range and on who would actually buy from you online. A shop brings ongoing work with it, a website doesn't.

Can I maintain the content myself later?

Yes. Text, images, products and prices are yours to change — set up, on request, so it works from a phone too.

What about data protection and GDPR?

Part of the build, not a surcharge. Form, cookies and consent are set up correctly from the start, imprint and privacy policy prepared.

Do you work outside Schwarzenbek and Hamburg?

Yes, across Germany. Usually video and email; on-site meetings around Hamburg are straightforward, travel costs billed separately.
